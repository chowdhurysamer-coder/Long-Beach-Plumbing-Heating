§ I. — the menu

Eight things we do, written plain.

No packages, no upsells. If it’s on this page we do it. If it’s not, call us anyway — it’s probably still a yes.

  1. 01

    Full-Service Irrigation

    Start-ups in the spring, mid-season checks, winterization in the fall, and repairs any time a head shears off.

  2. 02

    Lawn Maintenance

    Weekly cuts, edging, blow-downs. Same crew every visit so your yard isn’t a stranger to whoever’s holding the mower.

  3. 03

    Spring & Fall Clean-Ups

    Every last leaf, corner to corner. We bring our own tarps and we do not stop until the lawn looks like it’s yours again.

  4. 04

    Mulch, Top Soil & Seed

    Fresh beds, fresh lawn, fair rates. Dark mulch, hardwood, whatever you want — we’ll tell you what’ll actually last.

  5. 05

    Leveling

    Bumps, ruts, sunken sod, low spots that pool after the rain — flattened, topsoiled, reseeded.

  6. 06

    Gutter Cleaning

    Two-story homes, garages, the weird old downspout that wraps around the porch. All of it.

  7. 07

    Snow Removal

    Walks, drives, whole commercial lots. Salted, cleared, and done before your first coffee.

  8. 08

    Holiday Lights

    Installed on the eaves in November, maintained all season, taken down when you’re tired of looking at them.

Not on the list? Call anyway. If it lives outside, we probably handle it.
